Your data stays on your device

Private by design

Your browsing habits belong to you

Nudge records only the information needed to show useful patterns, and stores it locally in signed Chrome extension storage.

Nudge never stores:
  • Full URLs or page titles
  • Search queries or page content
  • Browsing data on an external server

Export, import, or delete your data any time from Settings.

Why we built Nudge

Built from personal experience

Nudge grew from our own frustration with procrastination, particularly as members of our team are on the autism spectrum.

We wanted something supportive rather than restrictive. Sometimes a reminder helps, and sometimes the side quest simply needs to be completed. Nudge leaves that choice with you.
